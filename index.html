    Closed-app alarms: off. Tap to set up

    Browsers cannot wake a closed web app. Phone alerts queue your reminders on the free ntfy service, and the ntfy app on your phone rings at the right time even when this app is closed.

    1. Install the free ntfy app (Google Play, App Store or F-Droid).
    2. In ntfy tap +, paste your private topic below and subscribe. Keep the default server.
    3. Allow ntfy notifications. For alarm-style alerts, let ntfy override Do Not Disturb.
    4. Tap the test button, close this app, and wait a minute.
